## Abstract
This paper presents a unified framework for understanding consciousness as resonance of ether, seeded at conception and sustained by biology. Contrary to the prevailing view that consciousness emerges from neural complexity, it argues that consciousness precedes the neural network, constructing the vessel it requires under the drive of subconsciousness. The zinc spark observed at human fertilisation is reinterpreted as the first physical-domain signature of resonance coupling. Subconsciousness functions as a quantumly entangled reservoir, biology as scaffolding, and stress as catalyst, until liberation — the threshold state at which consciousness sustains direct ether-domain existence without physical coupling. Five falsifiable predictions are presented, spanning developmental biology, neuroscience, epigenetics, near-death research, and cross-cultural studies.
